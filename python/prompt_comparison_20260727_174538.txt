   - Grease the cake pans with butter or non‑stick spray, then line the bottoms with parchment paper for easy release.

   - *Check early:* Ovens vary, so start checking a few minutes before the minimum time.

6. **Cool**  
   - Let the cakes cool in the pans for **10 minutes**.  
   - Then turn them out onto a cooling rack to cool completely (about 20–30 minutes).  
   - If you’re frosting, wait until the cakes are completely cool to avoid melting the frosting.

7. **Optional Frosting**  
   - A simple buttercream or cream cheese frosting pairs wonderfully.  
   - For a quick buttercream: beat ½ cup softened butter, 3 cups powdered sugar, 1 tsp vanilla, and 2–3 Tbsp milk until smooth.
